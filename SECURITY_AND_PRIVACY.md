# Security, Privacy & Portfolio Disclosure Policy

This is a public recruiting portfolio. It intentionally does **not** publish proprietary enterprise source code or sensitive business data.

## Never Published

- account credentials, cookies, session tokens or authentication artifacts;
- real customer IDs, inquiry messages or order records;
- real customer email / phone / WhatsApp lists collected for internal business use;
- internal settlement reports, cost tables or confidential finance data;
- company-only selectors, account-specific automation details or private operating procedures;
- personal information obtained from non-public sources.

## What May Be Published

- sanitized architecture descriptions;
- abstract workflow diagrams;
- synthetic sample inputs and outputs;
- simplified reference implementations that do not expose production logic or secrets;
- engineering decisions around validation, failure handling and human review;
- measurable outcomes that can be stated without exposing confidential data.

## Portfolio Principle

The goal is to demonstrate engineering thinking and delivery experience without turning a public GitHub repository into a copy of an employer's internal systems.
