## 数字怎么理解 / Metric interpretation

**约 15 工作日/月：** 原完整财务流程的项目估计。没有逐月前后工时对照，不能据此计算确定的净节省时间或效率百分比。

**~15 workdays/month:** a project estimate of the former complete finance workload. Without comparable before/after timing records, it is not a measured net saving or an efficiency percentage.

**约 200 条询盘或 200 笔订单：** 典型批次规模，不是日吞吐承诺。两项运营工具合计约 2 工作日/月为经验估计。

**~200 inquiries or orders:** typical batch size, not guaranteed daily throughput. The combined ~2 operations days saved per month is a project estimate.

**60 家目标企业：** 符合既定条件的研究交付，不是成交数。

**60 target companies:** qualified research deliveries, not converted sales.
